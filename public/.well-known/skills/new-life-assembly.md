# New Life Assembly of God

New Life Assembly of God is a welcoming Assemblies of God church at 47 Embry Acres Dr, Leitchfield, KY 42754, led by Pastor Tony Redmon since 1998. Website: https://www.newlifeaogleitchfield.com

## Service times

- Sunday School: Sundays at 10:00 AM
- Sunday Morning Service: Sundays at 11:00 AM (praise and worship, then preaching at 11:30 AM)
- Children's Church: Sundays at 11:30 AM
- Wednesday Bible Study: Wednesdays at 6:30 PM

## Helping someone visit

- Directions: [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=47+Embry+Acres+Dr,+Leitchfield,+KY+42754)
- There is no dress code; visitors are welcome to come as they are.
- Questions about a first visit are answered on the [Plan Your Visit page](https://www.newlifeaogleitchfield.com/contact).

## Contacting the church

- Pastor Tony Redmon: (270) 200-3422
- Church office: (270) 868-0369
- Messages: the contact form on the [Plan Your Visit page](https://www.newlifeaogleitchfield.com/contact) (name, email, and message required).

## Prayer requests

Use the form on the [Prayer Requests page](https://www.newlifeaogleitchfield.com/prayer). Only the request itself is required; a name can be left off to stay anonymous. Requests are emailed to the church and forwarded to Pastor Tony. Checking the confidential box marks the request for Pastor Tony only and keeps it from the church prayer circle; otherwise it may be shared with the prayer circle. Always let the person review a prayer request before it is sent.
